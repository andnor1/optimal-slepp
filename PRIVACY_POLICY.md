# Privacy Policy – Clock Out: Sleep Optimizer

**Last updated:** 2026-05-21

---

## 1. Overview

Clock Out ("the App", "we", "our") is built on a simple principle: your sleep data is yours alone. The App does not operate any servers, does not require an account, and does not transmit personal data to any third party. Everything the App collects stays on your device.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Microphone / Audio

The App requests microphone access solely to record audio during an active night recording session. Audio is processed locally on your device in real time to estimate sleep depth and generate a hypnogram. Raw audio is never written to disk, transmitted over the network, or shared with any party. Microphone access is active only while a recording session is running and stops immediately when you tap Stop or close the session.

### 2.2 Sleep and Activity Data

Sleep sessions you log manually or that result from a night recording are stored locally in the App's sandboxed storage using AsyncStorage. This includes:

- Sleep start and end times
- Duration and quality estimates
- Sleep phase data (deep, light, REM, awake)
- Nap entries and quality ratings
- Coaching progress and streak counts

None of this data leaves your device.

### 2.3 Apple Health (optional)

If you grant permission, the App may:

- **Read** heart rate and workout data from Apple Health to improve sleep phase detection during night recordings.
- **Write** completed sleep sessions to Apple Health as HKCategorySample sleep entries.

Health data accessed through HealthKit is used exclusively within the App for the purposes described above. It is never shared with third parties, advertisers, or any external service. You can revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy → Health.

### 2.4 Notifications

The App schedules local notifications (bedtime reminders, weekly reports, coaching tips) entirely on-device using Apple's notification framework. No notification content is routed through external servers.

### 2.5 Subscription Status

If you purchase a Premium subscription, the App uses RevenueCat to verify your subscription entitlement. RevenueCat may process your Apple ID purchase receipt to confirm active subscriptions. This is governed by [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). The App caches your subscription status locally and does not share any personally identifiable information with RevenueCat beyond what is required for purchase verification.

---

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, or any contact information.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.
- We do not track usage behaviour, screen views, or in-app events on any external server.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade any data.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All App data is stored in your device's local, sandboxed storage. It is protected by iOS's standard file system encryption and is included in your iCloud or iTunes device backup if you have enabled those services. Data in your backup is subject to Apple's privacy terms.

We do not maintain any databases, cloud infrastructure, or servers that hold your information.

---

## 5. How to Delete Your Data

You can delete all App data at any time:

1. Open the App → Profile tab → scroll to the bottom → **Delete all sleep history**.
2. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data permanently.
3. To remove sleep data written to Apple Health, open the Health app → Browse → Sleep → Data Sources & Access → Clock Out → Delete all data from this source.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

---

## 7. GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights regarding any personal data processed by the App:

- **Right of access** – you can inspect all data the App stores by reviewing the files in your device's App sandbox.
- **Right to erasure** – you can delete all data as described in Section 5.
- **Right to data portability** – the App provides an Export function (Analysis tab → Export) that exports your sleep history as a shareable image.
- **Right to object** – you can disable specific data inputs (microphone, Apple Health, notifications) in iOS Settings at any time.

Because the App does not transmit personal data to our servers, most GDPR obligations that apply to data controllers do not apply. The only external data processor is RevenueCat (Section 2.5), which processes only purchase receipt data.

Legal basis for processing: legitimate interest in providing the core sleep-optimisation features you have explicitly requested.

---

## 8. CCPA (California Users)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. The App does not sell personal information. All data remains on-device. To request deletion, follow the steps in Section 5.
