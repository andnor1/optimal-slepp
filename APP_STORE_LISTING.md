## Description

Tired of waking up exhausted even after a full eight hours? The problem isn't how long you sleep — it's *when* you sleep, and when your alarm cuts in.

Clock Out is built on the same circadian science used by sleep clinics. A personalised melatonin model tracks your body's natural rhythm and calculates the exact windows where you'll fall asleep fastest, sleep deepest, and wake up feeling human again. No generic advice. No one-size-fits-all schedules. Just your biology, working for you.

**How the science works**

Your brain releases melatonin on a 24-hour cosine curve anchored to your personal DLMO — Dim-Light Melatonin Onset. Most people's DLMO sits around 21:00, but shift workers, night owls, and frequent travellers can be off by two hours or more. Clock Out calibrates your personal DLMO from your sleep history and builds every recommendation around it. After three logged nights the model activates. After thirty it knows you better than any generic sleep guide ever could.

**Everything in Clock Out**

⏰ **Smart Alarm** — sets a wake window and listens for the lightest phase of your sleep cycle. Instead of jolting you from deep sleep, it wakes you at the moment you're closest to waking naturally. You'll notice the difference the first morning.

🎙️ **Night Recording** — the microphone analyses breathing patterns throughout the night to estimate sleep depth, detect cycles, and build a hypnogram you can review the next day. No wearable required.

📊 **Sleep Analysis** — a full history of your nights: duration, consistency, recovery score, and trend. A weekly sleep score grades your week A–F with breakdowns for duration, consistency, and coverage.

🧠 **Sleep Coaching** — five personalised tips per day drawn from a library of evidence-based advice on light, temperature, caffeine, exercise, screens, food, and stress. Each tip explains the *why* in plain language. Complete tips to build a streak. Take on weekly challenges.

✈️ **Jetlag Calculator** — enter your home timezone and destination. Clock Out generates a day-by-day adaptation plan that tells you when to seek light, when to avoid it, and shifts your bedtime and wake time by the right amount each day.

💤 **Power Nap Timer** — science-backed nap lengths (10, 20, 60, 90 minutes) timed to avoid sleep inertia. Rate how you feel after each nap to track what works for you.

🗓️ **Sleep Window Planner** — add upcoming sleep opportunities (travel, night shifts, early mornings) and let the algorithm find the optimal bedtime and cycle count for each one.

🍎 **Apple Health** — read heart rate from Apple Watch to improve phase detection. Write completed sleep sessions back to Health automatically.

🏅 **Badges** — earn milestones for consistency, perfect weeks, nap mastery, and early-bird mornings.

**Built for irregular schedules**

Shift workers, parents of newborns, frequent flyers, students pulling late nights — Clock Out was designed for people whose sleep doesn't follow the Monday-to-Friday template. Irregular Schedule Mode disables weekday assumptions and lets the algorithm work purely from your logged data.

**Your data stays on your device**

No account required. No cloud sync. Sleep data, recordings, and health information never leave your phone. Microphone access is requested only when you start a night recording and stops the moment you tap Stop.
